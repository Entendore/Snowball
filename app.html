❓ HOW TO PLAY

🎯 Goal: Roll your snowball to the bottom with the biggest size and highest score!

💎 Crystals: Collect to grow and score. Rarer = more points!

⚠️ Obstacles: Avoid dark crystals — they shrink you and cost lives.

⚡ Dash: SHIFT to dash through obstacles.

💥 Ultimate: Collect crystals to charge. Q to destroy nearby obstacles!

🛡️ Power-ups: Shield, Magnet, Speed, Multiplier, Life, Slow-Mo.

👹 Bosses: Each biome has a unique boss. Dash or ultimate to damage!

💰 Coins: Spend in shop for permanent upgrades.

⭐ Prestige: Reset for permanent score bonuses!

💥 Critical Hits: Random chance for 2x damage on obstacles!

📏 Size Matters: Bigger ball = stronger dash, bigger ultimate!
